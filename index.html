Biography

I am currently a ZJU 100 Young Professor in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University. Before joining Zhejiang University, I served as an Associate (tenured) Professor in the School of Software Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University. I received my Ph.D. from the Faculty of Information Technology at Monash University. I am very fortunate to have been advised by many outstanding researchers during my studies, including Prof. John Grundy, Dr. Jiangshan Yu, Prof. Xin Xia, Prof. Xiapu Luo and Prof. Ting Chen. My research interests focus on Agentic Economy, Web3 security, AI for Web3, Web3 for AI and software testing.

🔍 Looking for highly motivated students in the areas of AI for Web3, Web3 for AI, LLM Security, and Software Engineering. Please contact me if you have any interests.
